Trust Shield

Every listing we review gets a trust score from 0 to 100, built from verifiable signals — not vibes.

80+

Trustworthy

Checks out. Verified signals line up; proceed with normal caution.

50–79

Caution

Mixed signals. Worth a closer look before you invest your time.

0–49

Stay away

High risk. Verifiable red flags outweigh anything the listing promises.

We describe risk with facts you can check — never with labels you can't. A low score means "here's what we found," not a verdict on anyone's character.

How it works

  • Verifiable signals. Domain age, posting patterns, contact consistency, and other checkable facts — each one you could confirm yourself.
  • Rule engine first. Transparent rules make the call, so every score can be explained.
  • Machine learning, second opinion. Models advise; they don't overrule. No black-box verdicts.
  • Always improving. The system re-tests itself against known-good and known-bad listings, and flags when its own signals drift.

For employers

Think a score got it wrong? You get a fair hearing. Disputes are handled through a dedicated review channel — starting with email, moving to an on-site review form. We correct mistakes and show our work.

No listing is ever called a "scam" here. We publish risk levels and the verifiable facts behind them, and every employer can challenge the record.
